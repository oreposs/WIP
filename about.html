Our Story

Founded in 2014, Wedding Image Philippines is a trusted name in capturing life's most memorable moments. What started as Ma'am Wennie's passion for photography has grown into more than a decade of experience in weddings, corporate events, anniversaries, and special occasions.

Together with her husband Ariel and their dedicated team, they combine professionalism, creativity, and personal care to ensure every client feels valued and receives exceptional service that exceeds expectations.
